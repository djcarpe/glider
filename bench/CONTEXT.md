# `context` extension: what it costs and what it buys

Generated by `bench/context_bench.py` (driver) and `bench/context_report.py` from `bench/results/context.jsonl`. The harness is `bench/src/bin/ctxbench.rs`. Token counts use `glider::ext::context::tokens`, the estimate the extension budgets with (about one token per four characters of a word, one per symbol).

## Retrieval quality on a graph with a known answer

200,000 entities in communities of 50. Each entity links to 5 members of its own community, to 2 random entities anywhere (noise) and to one of 10 hubs; every edge has the same type, so only structure separates signal from noise. From a random seed, the relevant context is the seed's community. 100 seeds per budget. *Precision* is the share of shown entities (seed excluded) in the seed's community; *recall* the share of its 49 other members shown. Every method is rendered in the same compact text form except the two Cypher rows, which are what a model gets from a neighbourhood query returned as JSON.

- At 4,000 tokens about 90 nodes fit but the community has only 49 other members, so precision there is capped near 0.55; recall is what to read.
- "no hub damping" pushes PageRank mass through the hubs (`max_fanout` unlimited): about the same answer, 15–25× slower, because each push through a hub touches its tens of thousands of neighbours.
- The Cypher 1-hop answer shows about as many relevant nodes as `retrieve` does in 500 tokens, for ~10× the tokens, and cannot be asked for more; 2 hops is millions of tokens.

- `retrieve` touches a few thousand nodes whatever the graph's size (local push), so its cost is set by page reads, not by the 1.2M nodes; cold, most of the time is ~200 page misses.
- The generator's text comes from a vocabulary of ~50 words, so every query term is in most documents: the worst case for an inverted index. Each term's postings are read up to 50,000.
- The index is maintained by the write hook in the writing transaction; a `CREATE` of an indexed node stays well under a millisecond.
