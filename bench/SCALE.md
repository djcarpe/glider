# glider at scale: 1, 5, 10 and 25 GiB (snapshot-image engine)

> These are measurements of the engine before paged storage (a snapshot image plus an in-memory delta), kept as the baseline. The paged engine's numbers, and the comparison, are in [`PAGED.md`](PAGED.md).

Generated by `bench/scale.py` from files built by `scale-gen`; rendered by `bench/scale_report.py`. Every glider process ran under a 16 GiB address-space cap on a machine with 31 GiB of RAM (about 18 GiB free). "Old build" is the commit before snapshot images (11b80df), which replays the whole log on open. "Cold" means the file was evicted from the page cache first.

Built directly as snapshot images with `glider::image::build`, which streams properties to disk and keeps only a few integers per node and edge. The same generator also wrote the 1 GiB and 5 GiB graphs as plain logs for the old build; at 300k ids, replaying such a log and compacting it produced an image byte-identical to the direct build.

- log form of 1GiB: 0.91 GiB, written in 6.97 s at 14 MB peak RSS
- log form of 5GiB: 4.63 GiB, written in 30.95 s at 14 MB peak RSS
